## 1. What Is the Big Mystery in Physics?
For the last 100 years, theoretical physics has been divided into two separate, conflicting rulebooks:

1. **General Relativity (Einstein):** Explains massive things like gravity, stars, black holes, and the expanding universe. Spacetime is smooth and continuous.
2. **Quantum Mechanics (Bohr, Schrödinger):** Explains tiny things like atoms, electrons, and photons. Reality is discrete, probabilistic, and jumpy.

When physicists try to combine them to explain the very beginning (the Big Bang) or the very center of black holes, the equations break down. Furthermore, astronomers have had to invent two invisible "placeholders" that make up 95% of our cosmos:
* **Dark Matter:** An invisible substance supposed to provide extra gravity to keep galaxies together.
* **Dark Energy:** An unknown force supposed to be accelerating the expansion of space.

Neither has ever been directly detected in a laboratory.

### Pillar 1: The Big Bang and Inflation
In atomic physics, an electron in its resting state ($1s$) can absorb a particle of light (a photon) and jump up to an excited orbit ($2p$). When it does, its probability cloud rapidly expands and reorganizes.
* **In our universe:** That sudden geometric expansion *is* cosmic inflation and the Big Bang. We do not need to invent an imaginary "inflaton" particle; the expansion is driven by the geometry of the quantum jump itself.

### Pillar 2: Why the Universe Is 13.8 Billion Years Old
In laboratory physics, an electron stays in an excited state for only a billionth of a second ($10^{-9}$ seconds) before decaying. Why has our universe lasted 13.8 billion years?
* **Einstein's Time Dilation:** Because this hyper-particle carries enormous mass-energy, it creates an extreme gravitational well (similar to a black hole). From the outside, the jump takes a fraction of a nanosecond. But for observers trapped *inside* that gravitational well, time slows down so drastically that one nanosecond stretches into billions of years of cosmic history.

### Pillar 3: What Dark Matter Actually Is
Astronomers noticed that outer stars in galaxies rotate much faster than visible matter can explain, leading to the hypothesis of "Dark Matter."
* **In our universe:** Dark Matter is not an undiscovered particle. Using the **Schrödinger-Newton equations**, the paper shows that the quantum wave function of the hyper-electron has interference patterns—valleys and peaks of probability. These invisible gravitational valleys pull on stars, making them spin faster. It explains galactic rotation curves without adding non-existent particles.

### Pillar 4: Dark Energy and the End of Time
Astronomers also observe that the universe's expansion is accelerating, which they attribute to "Dark Energy."
* **In our universe:** An electron in an excited state is inherently unstable. As it nears the moment of spontaneous decay, its orbital geometry destabilizes. That instability is what we perceive as cosmic acceleration.
* **The Final Fate:** The universe will not freeze to death in a cold "heat death." It will end via **Spontaneous Emission**. The electron will drop back to its resting state, collapsing our internal spacetime in a flash and releasing all its energy as a single photon into the parent universe.

## 4. Why This Is Serious Science (Not Science Fiction)
Theoretical physics rejects speculation that cannot be tested. This paper is built on formal academic foundations:

1. **Orthodox Equations:** It does not invent new, unproven mathematics. It uses standard General Relativity (the Schwarzschild metric), standard Quantum Mechanics (the Schrödinger equation), and established orbital harmonics.
2. **Tested Against Real Data:** 
   * **CMB Light:** The paper models the temperature fluctuations in the Cosmic Microwave Background (Planck satellite data) as orbital harmonics, achieving a statistical fit of $\chi^2 / \mathrm{d.o.f.} = 1.04$.
   * **Galactic Rotation:** The model is tested against real galactic velocity curves from the SPARC astronomical database (such as galaxy NGC 3198), yielding a fit of $\chi^2 / \mathrm{d.o.f.} = 0.98$.
3. **Explicit Falsifiability:** The paper provides exact numerical boundaries. If future telescopes (such as next-generation CMB polarization surveys) find values outside these boundaries, the theory is formally falsified.
